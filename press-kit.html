🏢 About Digital Dichotomy

Digital Dichotomy is the research division of MT Tech Industries, investigating the intersection of AI, intellectual property, and human rights. The AIID (Architecture of AI Invention Disclosure) project exposes systemic extraction of inventor IP by AI platforms.
