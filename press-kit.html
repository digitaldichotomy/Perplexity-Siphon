🏢 About Digital Dichotomy

Digital Dichotomy is the research division of MT Tech Industries, investigating the intersection of AI, intellectual property, and human rights. The AIID (Architecture of AI Invention Disclosure) project exposes systematic IP extraction by AI platforms and advocates for inventor rights and regulatory accountability.

"Perplexity admits its platform is 'not a good idea' for sensitive IP, yet markets a 'Patents' feature inviting exactly that sharing. This isn't a bug—it's the architecture of extraction."
