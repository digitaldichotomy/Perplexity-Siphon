⚖️ Call for Legal Coalition

The AIID project seeks attorneys specializing in intellectual property, privacy law, consumer protection, and class-action litigation to pursue coordinated legal remedies against AI platforms engaged in systematic invention extraction.

This is not a single case but a pattern of practice requiring multi-jurisdictional coordination.

🎯 Target Areas

  • Deceptive Trade Practices - FTC Act §5 violations
  • Unfair Competition - State law violations
  • Privacy Violations - CCPA, GDPR, state laws
  • Patent Interference - USPTO coordination
  • Tortious Interference - Business relations
  • Conversion - Misappropriation of IP

📋 Case Elements

  • Documented admissions of data retention
  • Evidence of third-party sharing
  • Prior art creation through platform design
  • Material omissions in marketing
  • Asymmetric harm to individual inventors
  • Systematic (not incidental) extraction

🤝 Coalition Benefits

  • Shared evidence repository
  • Coordinated discovery strategy
  • Expert witness network
  • Media coordination
  • Regulatory filing support
  • Academic research collaboration

📚 Relevant Precedents & Frameworks

FTC v. Cambridge Analytica (2019)

Deceptive data harvesting practices. Established precedent for platform liability for undisclosed data use.

HiQ Labs v. LinkedIn (2022)

Data scraping and competitive use. Relevant to AI training data extraction questions.

Andersen v. Stability AI (2023)

AI training on copyrighted works without consent. Directly applicable to IP extraction.

35 U.S.C. § 102 - Prior Art

Statutory framework for patent invalidation through public disclosure. Platform design creates prior art.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Provides private right of action for unauthorized data sharing. Perplexity lawsuit filed under this theory.
