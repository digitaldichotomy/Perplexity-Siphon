📋 Provisional Patents

The provisional patent application (PPA) is your first line of defense:

💡 Key Advantage: A PPA establishes an early filing date (priority date) without triggering public disclosure. It's not examined and remains confidential for 12 months.

What to Include

  • Detailed written description of the invention
  • Drawings or diagrams (if applicable)
  • How to make and use the invention
  • Best mode contemplated by inventor

Filing Process

  1. Prepare specification (can be informal but thorough)
  2. File through USPTO.gov ($150 micro-entity, $300 small entity)
  3. Receive filing receipt with priority date
  4. Mark all materials "Patent Pending"
  5. File non-provisional within 12 months
⚠️ Critical Warning: Public disclosure before filing destroys foreign rights immediately. US has 1-year grace period, but most countries do not. File BEFORE any public discussion.

🔒 NDAs & Contracts

Before discussing with anyone (including "helpers"):

Essential NDA Elements

  • Definition of confidential information (broad)
  • Obligations of receiving party
  • Exceptions (publicly available, independently developed)
  • Term (typically 3-5 years minimum)
  • Return/destruction of materials
  • Remedies for breach
💡 Pro Tip: Use mutual NDAs even with "friendly" discussions. Free templates available from SCORE, local bar associations, or legalzoom.com.

Who Must Sign

  • Potential investors (before pitch)
  • Manufacturing partners
  • Software developers
  • Marketing consultants
  • ANYONE hearing details before patent filing

🤖 AI Safety Rules

Never forget: AI chats are surveillance, not secrecy.

🚫 NEVER Share With AI:
  • Novel mechanisms or technical implementations
  • Specific formulas, algorithms, or code
  • Proprietary business methods
  • Unfiled invention details
  • Trade secrets of any kind

Safe AI Use (High-Level Only)

💡 Placeholder Technique: If you must discuss specifics, use placeholders: "Component A connects to Process B using Method C." Never define what A, B, or C actually are.

👁️ Monitoring & Detection

Watch for extraction markers:

Red Flags in AI Responses

  • AI suddenly "understands" your specific mechanism after vague prompts
  • Responses include technical details you didn't provide
  • Platform suggests "improvements" to your undisclosed method
  • Competitors file similar patents shortly after your AI use
